How to publish a job

  1. Click Add New Job, fill every field (title, board, advt no, posts, qualification, age, fees, salary, state/location, dates, skills, notification text, documents, official apply URL, notification PDF link).
  2. Upload / paste the official notification file link in Notification PDF URL.
  3. Save — it appears instantly on Home, All Jobs and candidate dashboards.
