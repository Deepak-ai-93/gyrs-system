## Brand & Style
The design system establishes an authoritative, accessible, and high-velocity digital employment infrastructure for Gujarat’s youth. The visual language balances official statutory gravitas with the streamlined utility and information architecture of modern hiring portals. It rejects the antiquated, impenetrable aesthetic of legacy bureaucratic websites in favor of crisp visual hierarchy, decisive typographic signposts, and deliberate bilingual alignment.

The user interface targets aspiring job seekers, competitive exam applicants, and career counselors across both urban and rural contexts. The emotional posture must inspire unshakeable institutional trust, procedural transparency, and urgent motivation—eliminating friction, anxiety, and ambiguity surrounding qualification criteria, application deadlines, and external state examination routing (OJAS).

The aesthetic marries **Corporate / Modern Civic Utility** with tactical, high-information-density surfaces:
- **Canvas Discipline:** A strictly light-mode baseline constructed on subtle slate surfaces (`#F8FAFC`), framing pure-white floating container cards.
- **Bilingual Symbiosis:** Simultaneous pairing of Latin script with Gujarati script (`ગુજરાતી`), ensuring neither feels like an afterthought through matched x-heights, baseline rhythm, and visual weights.
- **Systematic Color Cues:** Restrained yet definitive utility colors (institutional blue, saffron highlights, status green, urgency crimson) that convey immediate administrative status without cognitive noise.

## Colors
The color palette relies on a rigorously defined light-only scheme that enforces administrative authority, high legibility, and critical state-level compliance.

### Brand & Functional Anchors
- **Primary Civic Blue (`#1E40AF`):** The primary color represents statutory authority and sovereign reliability. Applied to dominant navigation structures, active filter segments, primary interactive states, and top-tier branding elements. Hover state transitions to `#1E3A8A`; pressed transitions to `#172554`.
- **Saffron Action Accent (`#F97316`):** Inspired by traditional state heritage and high-visibility alerts. Reserved for urgent announcements, featured post markers, application status callouts, and key interaction nudges.
- **Statutory Success Green (`#16A34A`):** Designated exclusively for successful eligibility confirmations, fee clearance statuses, and the primary 'Apply on OJAS' outbound gateway actions. Darker variant `#15803D` handles text labels and interactive active states.
- **Deadline Crimson (`#DC2626`):** Dedicated to high-urgency thresholds—such as "Closing Soon" countdown counters, final application windows, mandatory compliance errors, and non-eligible qualification signals.

### Surface & Border Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#F8FAFC` - Slate 50):** Provides a calm, low-glare backdrop that reduces eye strain during prolonged application discovery sessions.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** All primary content containers, job cards, matrices, and form layers rest on pure white to maintain peak contrast.
- **Structural Outlines (`#E2E8F0` - Slate 200):** 1px boundary lines providing clear spatial containment for cards, tabular rows, and input surfaces without visual clutter.
- **Text & Contrast Tiers:**
  - **Heading & Heavy Labels (`#0F172A` - Slate 900):** Uncompromising contrast exceeding WCAG AAA compliance.
  - **Body & Primary Metadata (`#475569` - Slate 600):** Balanced for continuous reading across mixed screen calibrations.
  - **Secondary Captions & Placeholders (`#94A3B8` - Slate 400):** Tertiary details, inactive timestamps, and reference metadata.

## Typography
The system utilizes a dual-engine typographic framework designed to manage multi-script parity seamlessly. English display content leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** for modern architectural balance and geometric clarity. General body content, complex tabular data, and input UI rely on **Inter** for optimal micro-legibility at condensed scale.

### Bilingual Gujarati Integration
Whenever bilingual headings, job titles, or department badges appear (e.g., *GSRTC Helper - હેલ્પર*), the secondary script renders using **Noto Sans Gujarati** matching optical font-size and weight:
- Maintain an exact 1:1 weight pairing: Inter/Jakarta `600 (SemiBold)` pairs strictly with Noto Sans Gujarati `600 (SemiBold)`.
- Use an inline opacity or color reduction (`#475569` or `85%` opacity) on the secondary language string to maintain a unified visual hierarchy without competing for visual dominance.
- Vertical alignment between Latin characters and Gujarati script must adhere to the shared middle baseline, compensating for the matra (top-stroke) accents of Gujarati without inducing line clipping.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system enforces a structured 12-column grid system tailored for dense information display, side-by-side verification workflows, and persistent multi-facet filtering.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1280px and wider):** Fixed maximum content width of 1440px with a 12-column grid. Filter sidebars lock to a rigid 3-column span (280px–320px width), leaving 9 columns allocated to job feeds, tabular matrices, and candidate profiles. Margins are fixed at `2rem`, gutters at `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. The filter sidebar collapses into a sliding off-canvas drawer triggered by a sticky filter bar. Margins contract to `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout. Single-column stacked stream. Sidebars convert into full-screen bottom-sheet overlays with sticky bottom action controls. Margins and gutters lock to `1rem`.

### Spatial Rhythm
Internal element spacing adheres strictly to an 8-point harmonic grid (using `space-xs` (4px) exclusively for tight label pairings, badge internal paddings, and table cell adjustments). Component paddings prioritize scanning speed: job listing cards utilize `space-lg` internally, while compact table rows and metadata tags rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm` for high information throughput.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is maintained using a soft ambient approach that conveys clear physical layering without heavy, dated drop shadows. Depth establishes priority across static feed cards, interactive sidebars, and critical overlays.

### Shadow Tokens & Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Inline):** Standard structural elements like borders (`border-slate-200`) and tabular cells use zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Feed Cards & Inputs):** Default state for job listing cards and search blocks:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Dropdowns):** Used when hovering over cards, opening filter dropdowns, and elevating status summary blocks:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers & Utility Bars):** Reserved for the sticky top government helpline bar and main navigation:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`
- **Level 4 (Floating Modals & Bottom Sheets):** Modal dialogs, application checklists, and notification popovers:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`

### Glass & Blur Elements
Sticky navigation headers utilize a light translucent frosted backplate: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92); backdrop-filter: blur(8px);` accompanied by an explicit bottom boundary (`border-b border-slate-200`).

## Shapes
The design system operates at roundedness level **2**, striking a balance between accessible approachability and institutional order.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem):** Standard form controls, input fields, dropdown menus, button groups, and alert callouts utilize an 8px radius.
- **Cards & Primary Modules (`rounded-2xl` / 1rem):** Core job cards, statistical display cards, tabular matrix containers, and sidebar filter cards utilize a generous 16px corner radius to produce clean visual separation.
- **Tag Chips & Badges (`rounded-full`):** Deadline countdowns, bilingual department identifiers, qualification labels, and active status tags utilize pill shapes (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata from interactive rectangular buttons.

## Components

### 1. Sticky Government Helpline & Navigation Bar
- **Top Utility Helpline Bar:** Stays affixed above the main header. Height: 36px. Background: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) or `#1E3A8A`. Text: `#F8FAFC`. Features official Gujarat State Emblem lockup, bilingual accessibility toggles (English / ગુજરાતી), high-contrast toggle, and live toll-free candidate helpline (`1800-XXX-XXXX`).
- **Main Nav Bar:** Height: 72px. Sticky positioning beneath the helpline bar with Level 3 elevation. Contains dual branding (`GYRS` + Gujarat State Seal), real-time search trigger, notification center, and Candidate Profile/SSO login button.

### 2. Job Listing Cards (`rounded-2xl`)
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and Level 1 elevation, shifting to Level 2 on hover with a subtle `2px` left accent border in `#1E40AF`.
- **Card Anatomy:**
  - Header: Job title in bold English and secondary Gujarati (`GSRTC Traffic Inspector - ટ્રાફિક ઇન્સ્પેક્ટર`), department code, and government posting logo.
  - Urgent Ribbon/Badge: Placed in the top-right corner. If days remaining ≤ 3, use Crimson (`#DC2626`) badge with an animated clock icon; otherwise Saffron (`#F97316`) for standard windows.
  - Metrics Grid: Remuneration (Seventh Pay Matrix), total vacancies, posting region, and educational minimums in compact labeled blocks.
  - Footer Action Bar: Bookmark action, detailed syllabus link, and primary action routing.

### 3. 'Apply on OJAS' Button & Outbound Indicators
- Specialized statutory action button styled in Emerald Green (`#16A34A` background, `#FFFFFF` text).
- Includes an explicit external link diagonal arrow indicator (`↗`) with a micro-tooltip: *"Redirects to Official OJAS Portal (ojas.gujarat.gov.in)"*. Hover shifts to `#15803D`.

### 4. Tag Chips & Countdown Badges
- **Countdown Badge:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `4px 10px`. Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#DC2626`, bold typography (`label-sm`).
- **Category & Quota Chips:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.

### 5. Tabular Eligibility & Fee Matrices
- Dense tabular layout contained within a `rounded-2xl` shell with an explicit border.
- Alternating subtle row zebra striping: `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`.
- Table header: `#0F172A` text, `#F1F5F9` background, uppercase `label-xs` tracking.
- Columns for General, SEBC/OBC, SC, ST, and EWS tiers with verified tick icons (`#16A34A`) or exemption waivers clearly marked.

### 6. Interactive Filter Sidebar
- Persistent 300px panel comprising segmented accordions for: Department, District (Ahmedabad, Surat, Rajkot, etc.), Educational Level (10th/12th Pass, Diploma, Graduate), and Salary Tiers.
- Search input inside filter groups for rapid multi-checkbox selection.
- Clear All and Apply Filters pinned at the bottom on mobile viewports.

### 7. Alert Toggle Switches (WhatsApp & Email)
- Micro-switch components allowing candidates to subscribe to instant vacancy notifications.
- Active switch state: Primary Blue (`#1E40AF`) or WhatsApp Green (`#22C55E`).
- Paired with explicit SMS/WhatsApp iconography and legal privacy assurance badge.

### 8. Official State Footer
- Full-width dark container (`#0F172A`).
- Contains official disclaimers confirming data parity with Gujarat Government Gazettes, external link policies, NIC hosting credits, and statutory disclaimers indicating the system acts as a unified registration and notice gateway.
